Pack Ops Privacy Policy

Updated November 25, 2026. Pack Ops is the private business application operated by 1290763 BC Ltd for its authorized company users. This notice covers the application and its connected services.

Information used

Pack Ops stores information entered by authorized company users, including customer and job details, quotes, time, materials, photos and business documents.

When you connect Gmail, Pack Ops requests read-only Gmail access. Its importers search for relevant financial emails and supplier invoices and read message identifiers, sender details, subjects, dates, message content and attachments needed for document intake and material-price updates. The Google permission technically permits reading the connected mailbox; the importers use configured searches to select relevant messages.

QuickBooks Online connection

Only the Pack Ops owner can connect the company’s QuickBooks Online account through Intuit’s authorization screen. Pack Ops receives connection credentials and reads invoice information, including customer names, invoice identifiers and numbers, dates, currency, totals, tax and remaining balances, to compare invoices and explicitly link matching records.

Intuit’s accounting permission allows broader accounting access, including writes. The initial Pack Ops integration only reads QuickBooks accounting records. It does not create or send invoices, process payments, or automatically change the company’s accounting records. New capabilities will require an updated description and appropriate authorization before use.

QuickBooks access credentials are encrypted before storage and are not returned to the browser. QuickBooks information is used for the company’s invoice comparison, not advertising, sale, or AI model training.

Addresses and driving distances

When address suggestions or driving-distance calculations are used, the address search text or route addresses are sent to Google Maps services to return suggestions and distances.

Purpose and storage

Imported invoice PDFs, email provenance, extracted invoice lines and price history are stored in the company’s Pack Ops account. Google access and refresh credentials are encrypted in the application database. They allow scheduled imports without leaving a browser open. Pack Ops uses Supabase for its database, private document storage and import processing, and Cloudflare Pages to host the app. The current Supabase project is hosted in the United States. Service providers may process information outside Canada under their applicable terms and safeguards.

Access and sharing

Imported records are available to authorized company users according to application permissions. Data is processed by the hosting and storage providers to operate these features. The Gmail importers do not send email, change mailbox messages, use email for advertising, sell Google user data, or send invoice content to an AI extraction service.

Pack Ops’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your controls

Disconnecting QuickBooks in Pack Ops revokes the connection and removes its stored credentials, imported QuickBooks comparisons and matching links. Original Pack Ops invoices and other company records are not deleted by that action. You can also revoke access in QuickBooks. Contact the administrator below for removal requests involving previously imported information or backups.

Disconnect Gmail in Pack Ops to stop use of that connection. You can also revoke Pack Ops access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Disconnecting does not automatically delete previously imported documents, price history or company business records. Those records remain available for business use until removed through the app or by its administrator; backups may retain copies under the storage provider’s retention settings.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy requests, contact the Pack Ops administrator at pack@packelectrical.com. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.
