Pack Ops Privacy Policy

Updated October 27, 2026. This notice describes the Pack Ops application used by Pack Construction and its Gmail integration.

Information used

Pack Ops stores information entered by authorized company users, including customer and job details, quotes, time, materials, photos and business documents.

When you connect Gmail, Pack Ops requests read-only Gmail access. Its importers search for relevant financial emails and supplier invoices and read message identifiers, sender details, subjects, dates, message content and attachments needed for document intake and material-price updates. The Google permission technically permits reading the connected mailbox; the importers use configured searches to select relevant messages.

Purpose and storage

Imported invoice PDFs, email provenance, extracted invoice lines and price history are stored in the company’s Pack Ops account. Google access and refresh credentials are encrypted in the application database. They allow scheduled imports without leaving a browser open. Pack Ops uses Supabase for its database, private document storage and import processing, and Cloudflare Pages to host the app.

Access and sharing

Imported records are available to authorized company users according to application permissions. Data is processed by the hosting and storage providers to operate these features. The Gmail importers do not send email, change mailbox messages, use email for advertising, sell Google user data, or send invoice content to an AI extraction service.

Pack Ops’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your controls

Disconnect Gmail in Pack Ops to stop use of that connection. You can also revoke Pack Ops access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Disconnecting does not automatically delete previously imported documents, price history or company business records. Those records remain available for business use until removed through the app or by its administrator; backups may retain copies under the storage provider’s retention settings.

For access, correction, deletion or privacy requests, contact the Pack Ops administrator at pack@packelectrical.com. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.
