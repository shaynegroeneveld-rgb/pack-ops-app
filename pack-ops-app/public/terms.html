Pack Ops — Use and Support

Updated November 25, 2026. Pack Ops is a private business application operated by 1290763 BC Ltd for its own authorized company users. The company permits those users to use Pack Ops for authorized company work while their access remains approved. It is not offered as a public subscription service. Access requires a Pack Ops account authorized by the company. Google authorization connects an eligible mailbox; it does not grant access to company records.

Use the app and its Gmail connection only for company work and data you are authorized to access. Review imported information and exceptions before relying on it for purchasing, billing or accounting decisions.

The Gmail integration reads relevant business documents and supports automatic supplier-price updates. It does not send mail or change the connected inbox. You can disconnect it in Pack Ops or revoke access through Google.

QuickBooks Online

Only the company’s authorized owner may connect its QuickBooks account. The initial connection reads invoices for comparison and allows explicit links to Pack Ops invoices. It does not send invoices, move money, or replace QuickBooks accounting records. Saved comparisons reflect the time they were checked; refresh and review discrepancies before relying on them.

Connecting QuickBooks does not create a Pack Ops account or give another person access to company information. Use your own authorized sign-in, protect your device and credentials, and report unexpected access to the administrator. The company may withdraw access when it is no longer authorized.

Intuit’s services remain subject to Intuit’s applicable terms. Pack Ops is not an Intuit product. Disconnecting the integration removes imported comparisons and links as described in the privacy policy; it does not delete the company’s original invoices.

For support or access questions, contact pack@packelectrical.com. See the privacy policy for data handling and removal requests.
