Pack Ops — Use and Support

Pack Ops is a private business application for authorized Pack Construction users. Access requires a Pack Ops account authorized by the company. Google authorization connects an eligible mailbox; it does not grant access to company records.

Use the app and its Gmail connection only for company work and data you are authorized to access. Review imported information and exceptions before relying on it for purchasing, billing or accounting decisions.

The Gmail integration reads relevant business documents and supports automatic supplier-price updates. It does not send mail or change the connected inbox. You can disconnect it in Pack Ops or revoke access through Google.

For support or access questions, contact pack@packelectrical.com. See the privacy policy for data handling and removal requests.
