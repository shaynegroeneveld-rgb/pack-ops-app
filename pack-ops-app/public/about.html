Pack Ops

Pack Ops is the private job-management app used by Pack Construction. Authorized team members manage quotes, jobs, time, materials and business documents.

Gmail connection

An authorized owner or office user can connect a business Gmail account. Pack Ops reads matching invoice emails and attachments to import financial documents and update material costs from E.B. Horsman invoices. Scheduled imports can run while the app is closed.

The Gmail permission is read-only. Connecting Gmail does not grant permission to send mail, delete messages or change your inbox. Google authorization alone does not create a Pack Ops user account or grant access to company jobs.
